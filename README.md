**Professional business email hosting, made simple.**

MailAfiniti is a hosted email platform for small businesses that want a professional you@yourcompany.com address without paying for a full office suite.

---

## What we do

We run custom-domain email for small businesses, freelancers, and growing teams:

- **Custom domain email**: yourname@yourbusiness.com, with inbox, calendar, and contacts
- **99%+ inbox delivery**: messages land in the inbox, not the spam folder
- **Guided SPF, DKIM, and DMARC setup**: we generate the DNS records, you paste them into your registrar
- **Spam and threat filtering**: powered by Rspamd
- **From $1.50/mailbox/month**: billed annually, on the Starter plan (10 GB)

## Why MailAfiniti

Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 cost four to five times more because you're paying for an office suite, not just email. If you already have your documents and calendar sorted, most of that bill is for software you don't use.

MailAfiniti does one thing: business email that arrives. Plans scale from Starter (10 GB) to Professional (30 GB) and Enterprise (50 GB), with free migration from your current provider on Professional and above.

Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with no card required, and a 30-day money-back guarantee.
